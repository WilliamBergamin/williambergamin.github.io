Bone Marrow

Ingredients

1 pers.

Instructions

  1. Clean the large bones and place them in the sous vide at 155F for 1 hour;
  2. Cut a 5-inch piece out of your baguette and slice in half length-wise;
  3. Drizzle the two halves with olive oil and rub with the garlic clove;
  4. Once the sous vide is done, grill the inside of the bread pieces in a hot pan;
  5. Empty the bone marrow from the bones onto the bread and spread like butter;
  6. Add the minced chives and fleur de sel.

Bread

Ingredients

1 loaf

Sourdough Starter

Bread

Instructions

Sourdough Starter

  1. Take a clean glass jar with a lid;
  2. Add 3 tbsp of water and 3 tbsp of white flour in a clean jar and stir;
  3. Wait 24 hours;
  4. Add 1 tbsp of water and 1 tbsp of white flour in the jar;
  5. Repeat from step 3 until the jar is 3/4 full;
  6. If the jar smells very very bad throw it away and restart.

Bread

  1. In a large bowl add the sourdough starter, water, salt and white flour;
  2. Mix with the end of a wooden spoon until fully incorporated;
  3. Let the dough rise for 8 to 12 hours;
  4. Pour the dough on a clean counter covered in flour;
  5. Fold the four corners of the dough;
  6. Flip the dough over;
  7. Tighten the dough with a spatula;
  8. Place the dough on a piece of parchment paper;
  9. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes;
  10. Place a dutch oven with the lid in the oven;
  11. Heat up the oven to 475F;
  12. Cut the parchment paper around the dough;
  13. Place the dough and the parchment paper in the dutch oven, with the lid on;
  14. Place the dutch oven in the oven at 475F for 15 minutes;
  15. Turn down the oven to 450F for 15 minutes;
  16. Take the lid off the dutch oven and set the oven to 440F for 15 minutes;
  17. Take the bread out, let it rest on a wire rack for 2 hours;
  18. Slice and enjoy.

Crostoli

Ingredients

About 100 crostoli

Instructions

Dough

  1. On a clean surface, place the flour in the shape of a bowl;
  2. Add the eggs, egg yolk, sugar, butter, brandy, vanilla and salt in the middle;
  3. With a fork or your fingertips, gradually work the flour into the wet ingredients until the dough comes together. Add a splash more brandy if it won't form a ball;
  4. Knead for about 5 minutes until the dough is smooth, elastic and soft without being sticky;
  5. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes. Tip: with a stand mixer, combine everything with the paddle on low, then knead with the dough hook.

Rolling and Shaping

  1. Cut a small piece off the dough and flatten it;
  2. Pass it through the pasta machine on the widest setting, folding and passing it 5 or 6 times until smooth. Dust with flour as needed;
  3. Step the setting down until the thinnest one; the dough should be nearly transparent (pass it twice on the thinnest setting if needed);
  4. Cut the dough into strips about 5 cm wide and 10-15 cm long;
  5. Make a small cut in the middle of each strip and pull one end through it;
  6. Keep the shaped crostoli covered so they don't dry out before frying.

Frying

  1. In a heavy, high-sided pan, heat the oil to 350F (180C);
  2. Fry 3 or 4 crostoli at a time, about 30 seconds per side. Flip them with two forks once lots of bubbles form;
  3. Take them out when light golden; if they are brown they are burnt. If they take too long to cook, the oil is not hot enough and they will turn out greasy;
  4. Drain on paper towels; they will crisp up as they cool;
  5. When cool, dust generously with powdered sugar;
  6. Keep in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks.

Fish Tacos

Ingredients

2 pers.

Instructions

  1. Mince the french onions, mango, cilantro (keep some for garnish), cherry tomatoes and place in a bowl. Add some olive oil, salt and pepper and set aside;
  2. In a medium heated pan, pour the beer and let it boil for 2-3 minutes, then add the salted butter. Lower the heat and mix with a wooden spoon; then add the fish and cover the pan with a lid. Cook the fish in the beer for 10-15 minutes (until soft);
  3. Remove the fish and deglaze the pan;

Fresh Pasta

Ingredients

3 pers.

Instructions

  1. On a clean surface, place the flour in the shape of a bowl;
  2. Place the olive oil, eggs and egg yolks in this flour bowl;
  3. With a fork, gradually mix the egg mixture with the flour until everything is combined;
  4. With your hands, continue mixing everything until it creates a uniform dough;
  5. Work this dough by folding it over multiple times (the more you work your dough, the more elasticity it will have and the chewier your pasta will be);
  6. Let the dough rest for at least an hour;
  7. Cut the dough into smaller pieces and pass it through your pasta machine;
  8. Form the type of pasta you want and enjoy.

Goose Sous Vide

Ingredients

1 pers.

Instructions

  1. Mince the french onion;
  2. Roast the french onion in duck fat until caramelized;
  3. Place all ingredients in a Zip-Loc bag and mix;
  4. Submerge the Zip-Loc bag in a sous-vide tub;
  5. Cook for 2 hours at 130F;
  6. Open the Zip-Loc bag and in a warm cast iron pan, sear the meat until grilled on either side;
  7. Set aside the meat and empty the contents of the Zip-Loc bag in the pan;
  8. Deglaze the pan with white wine and cook until the mix thickens;
  9. Serve the meat with the seasonings and preferably with white rice and/or greens.

Hot Sauce

Adapted from Joshua Weissman, with xanthan gum added.

Ingredients

About 750 ml

Fermented Peppers

Hot Sauce

Instructions

  1. Remove the pepper stems and slice the peppers in half lengthwise;
  2. Dissolve the sea salt in the filtered water to make the brine;
  3. Place the peppers in a 2 L mason jar, cover with the brine and keep them submerged with a weight. Close the lid;
  4. Ferment at room temperature for 5 days to 2 weeks;
  5. Strain the peppers through a mesh strainer, keeping the brine;
  6. Peel and thinly slice the garlic. In a saucepan on medium-low heat, gently toast it in the oil until golden brown;
  7. Strain the garlic through a fine mesh strainer, keeping the garlic and the oil separate;
  8. In a blender, add the toasted garlic, fermented peppers, vinegar and brine. Blend on high until smooth;
  9. With the blender running on low, sprinkle in the xanthan gum slowly so it doesn't clump, then blend on high for 30 seconds;
  10. With the blender running, stream in half of the garlic oil;
  11. Add kosher salt to taste and let the sauce rest for 10 minutes to fully thicken.

Pho Soup

Ingredients

3 pers.

Broth

Toppings

Tofu

Instructions

  1. Start by preparing the broth. Grate the ginger using a zester and cut the lemongrass in small pieces. Set everything in a cauldron and set to medium heat;
  2. Secondly, we recommend to prepare the tofu and the marinade for it to be ready to be roasted later. Chop the tofu in 1-cm cubes, mix all the liquids together and set aside. Keep the sesame seeds aside;
  3. Chop the shiitake mushrooms and napa cabbage and roast on medium heat in a pan with a drizzle of sesame oil;
  4. Set the veggies aside;
  5. Prepare the tofu:
    1. Put the stove on medium heat;
    2. In a pan (preferably a wok), add a drizzle of sesame oil;
    3. Add the tofu to the pan when hot;
    4. Roast it until the edges are golden;
    5. Turn off the stove and add the marinade;
    6. Add the sesame seeds.
  6. To prepare the toppings, mince the green onions and soft-boil the eggs:
    1. Boil a pot of water;
    2. Add the eggs;
    3. Cook for 7 minutes;
    4. Drain and plunge the eggs in a cold-water bath for 1 minute;
    5. Peel the eggs and cut in half.
  7. Boil some water and add the vermicelli when boiling, cook for 3 minutes or as indicated on the packaging;
  8. Add the vermicelli to an individual bowl with the broth, add the toppings;
  9. Top with cilantro and a halved-lime.

Roasted Gnocchi

Ingredients

2 pers.

Instructions

  1. First, peel the potatoes. Then, bring water to a boil and add some salt to it. Drop in the potatoes and cook until soft;
  2. Mash the potatoes and let them rest for a little bit for them to cool down;
  3. Mix the mashed potatoes with the flour in a big bowl, then let it rest for an hour;
  4. From the dough ball that you just made, separate some pieces and make them into long strings;
  5. Then, cut the strings in small cubes and smoosh them with a fork for them to look like a gnocchi;
  6. When all the dough is transformed into gnocchi, bring water to a boil again, add some salt and drop in the gnocchi. Note: If you feel you have too much gnocchi for your meal, you can easily freeze them by putting parchment paper between them;
  7. Cook until all the gnocchi come to the surface; that will mean they are ready;
  8. Lastly, in a pan, put some olive oil and roast the freshly cooked gnocchi. This way, they will be crunchy on the outside and chewy on the inside. We recommend to enjoy with our homemade pesto.

Sugar Pie

Ingredients

1 pie (9")

Instructions

  1. Bake the pie crust as directed on the box, but do not prick it with a fork or the sugar filling will leak through;
  2. Meanwhile, mix all the filling ingredients until the sugar is dissolved;

  5. Serve to taste.

Tartare

Ingredients

1 pers.

Instructions

  1. In a big bowl, mix the liquids with sesame seeds, wasabi powder;
  2. Mince the cilantro, french onion, black garlic, ginger and hot pepper, then add to the big bowl;
  3. Zest the lime and then squeeze it, add to the big bowl;
  4. Chef's special trick: buy some sushi-grade fish, but frozen. Cut the fish in small cubes while it is thawing;
  5. Mix the fish with the seasoning;
  6. Add the fleur de sel to taste;
  7. Serve with thinly sliced baguette toasted in the oven.

Tomato Sauce

Ingredients

6 pers.

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic;
  2. In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat and cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes;
  3. Add the garlic, tomato paste and herbes de Provence, and stir for 1 to 2 minutes;
  4. Optionally, add the vodka or wine and let it reduce by half, about 2 minutes;
  5. Add the tomatoes, crushing them with a spoon, bring to a simmer and cook for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally;
  6. Add salt to taste.

Tomato Soup

Ingredients

6 pers.

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic;
  2. In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat and cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes;
  3. Add the garlic, tomato paste and herbes de Provence, and stir for 1 to 2 minutes;
